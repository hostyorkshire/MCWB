Send Weather Commands

Want weather updates on your MeshCore network? Here's how to use the bot!

No Installation Needed!

You don't need to install anything — just send a message on the MeshCore app and the bot will reply with current weather conditions instantly!

How to Send Commands

Step 1: Open your MeshCore app or client

Step 2: Type your weather command

Step 3: Send the message on any channel where the bot is listening

That's it! The bot will respond with current weather conditions for your requested location.

Command Syntax

Use either wx or weather followed by a location:

wx [location]
weather [location]

Examples

wx London
wx Manchester
wx New York USA

Case Insensitive

Commands are case-insensitive — WX London, wx london, and Wx London all work exactly the same.

What You'll Get Back

The bot replies with current weather conditions including:

  • Current temperature and conditions
  • "Feels like" temperature
  • Humidity percentage
  • Wind speed and direction

Example Response

wx London
London, GB
Partly cloudy
Temp: 14.2°C (feels 12.8°C)
Humid: 72%
Wind: 18 km/h at 230°

Tips & Tricks

Disambiguation with Country Codes

Some city names exist in multiple countries. Add a country code to get the right one:

wx York UK
York, GB (United Kingdom)
wx York USA
York, US (United States)

What Locations Work?

  • City names — wx Sheffield, wx Tokyo
  • City + country — wx Paris FR, wx Sydney AU
  • UK postcodes — wx S1 2HH, wx S71
  • Landmarks — many well-known places work too

Any Channel Works

Commands work on any channel the bot is monitoring. You don't need to be on a specific channel — just send on whatever channel you normally use.

Common Issues

Not getting a response? Check the following:

Issue What to Check
No response at all Is the bot running/online? Ask a network admin to check.
Location not found Check the spelling, or try adding a country code (e.g. wx York UK)
Wrong location returned Use a country code to disambiguate (e.g. wx Springfield USA)
Command not recognised Make sure there is a space after wx — wxLondon won't work

For more detailed help, see the Troubleshooting Guide.
